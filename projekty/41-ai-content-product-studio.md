# Project 41 — AI Content Product Studio

## Thesis

Convert the supplied content-creation and digital-product material into a repeatable production system rather than a collection of prompts.

## Product pipeline

`research → audience model → offer → editorial plan → generation → QA → packaging → human approval → publishing → measurement`

## Initial product families

- blog/article systems;
- email and newsletter sequence packs;
- social-content systems;
- digital download/template packs;
- niche-specific communication kits.

## Quality controls

Every generated artifact should pass:

- factual/source check where applicable;
- brand/style consistency;
- duplication detection;
- legal/compliance checklist;
- accessibility/readability review;
- human approval before external publication.

## Business model experiments

- one-off template packs;
- subscription libraries;
- workflow-as-a-service;
- verticalized professional packs;
- custom content operations.

## Success metrics

- production time per approved artifact;
- revision cycles;
- approval rate;
- conversion or engagement where ethically measurable;
- reuse rate of assets/workflows;
- human review minutes per published unit.
