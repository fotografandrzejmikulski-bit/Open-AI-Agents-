# Project 107 — LUMEN Documentary Photography Intelligence Lab MAX

## Status
PROPOSED → ARCHITECTURE BASELINE → PROJECT GENESIS 2026-09-10

## Mission
Create a specialized visual-intelligence and prompt-engineering laboratory for street photography, photojournalism and documentary photography, combining photographic theory, optics, film/emulsion characteristics, composition, ethics and generative-image prompt construction.

## Distinct boundary
P07 owns broad agentic creative production and P97 owns recursive multimedia production. P107 owns the **photographic language and documentary-image intelligence layer**: genre semantics, historical visual grammar, photographic parameters and high-fidelity prompt engineering.

## Photographic parameter system
Prompt schemas should encode:
- genre and documentary intent;
- subject/context;
- camera class;
- focal length and perspective;
- aperture/depth of field;
- shutter behavior and motion rendering;
- ISO/noise/grain;
- light source and direction;
- color temperature;
- composition and decisive moment;
- film/emulsion or digital rendering characteristics;
- environment and spatial geometry;
- ethical/contextual constraints.

The source provides concrete film/camera examples including Kodak Tri-X 400, Cinestill and Ilford Pan F 50, with distinct grain, contrast, halation and tonal characteristics. fileciteturn907file2L117-L189

## Master reference layer
The source treats historical photographers as visual-language anchors and discusses figures including Cartier-Bresson, Robert Frank, Garry Winogrand, Dorothea Lange, Alex Webb, Martin Parr, Chris Niedenthal and Tadeusz Rolke. fileciteturn907file4L250-L260

P107 uses historical references as **descriptive photographic attributes**, not as instructions to impersonate living artists or falsely attribute generated work.

## Documentary ethics gate
The system must distinguish:
- observed/documentary claim;
- artistic reconstruction;
- synthetic scene;
- historical reference;
- fictional image.

Generative images must never be presented as authentic documentary evidence of an event that did not occur. Source provenance and generation status remain attached to every artifact.
