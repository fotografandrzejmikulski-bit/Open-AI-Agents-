# Project 82 — CCR Transmedia Graphic-Novel Universe MAX

## Status
PROPOSED → ARCHITECTURE BASELINE → PROJECT GENESIS 2026-09-10

## Mission
Transform the complete CCR trilogy into a production-grade graphic-novel and transmedia universe in which light, memory, consciousness and nonlinear time are expressed through sequential art, typography, physical print effects and optional AR layers.

## Why this is new
Project 73 is a game-specific Człowiek Roku / evidence-driven narrative project. The supplied CCR document defines a much larger graphic-novel product: a multi-timeline universe, character system, visual grammar, pilot volume, production specification and AR/transmedia layer. The complete graphic-novel deliverable therefore deserves an independent lineage.

## Core visual thesis
```text
LIGHT
 ↓
MEMORY
 ↓
CONSCIOUSNESS
 ↓
TIMELINE
 ↓
PANEL / PAGE / VOLUME
```

Light is treated as a semantic narrative element. Different timelines receive different visual identities rather than a single universal palette.

## Timeline grammar
- 1679 — historical/alchemical chiaroscuro, engraving-like linework.
- 1983 — Polish analog brutalism and fluorescent realism.
- 2025 — Cieszyn / Silesian Noir and grounded photography-derived composition.
- 2135 — CCR laboratory, fluid frames, resonance and luminous abstraction.
- 2345 — Neo-Paris baroque cyberpunk and olfactory synesthesia.
- 2777 — Devil Mind, fractured/glitch visual grammar.
- Infinity — open visual field representing persistence of light.

## Pilot architecture
The supplied pilot begins in Cieszyn in 2025, moves into a mining-memory sequence and then to a 2135 CCR laboratory, ending with the Shadow. This sequence becomes a reference implementation for timeline transitions, negative/positive imagery, silence, memory and cliffhanger construction.

## Visual grammar
Panel boundaries are part of the story. Grounded timelines use stable grids; CCR/alternative timelines may use fluid frames; 2777 may use fractured/open-canvas composition. Equations, holographic interfaces and sound/light concepts are treated as diegetic visual language rather than unexplained decoration.

## Transmedia layer
Optional AR can recognize declared pages and expose additional audio/light animation/lore. AR content must be versioned against the print edition and remain optional; a failed network connection cannot invalidate the physical book.

## Provenance boundary
The adaptation must distinguish original source material, adaptation choices, visual inventions, speculative scientific language and externally sourced factual claims. Equations used as narrative devices are not automatically scientific validation.

## Quality gates
1. Canon consistency.
2. Timeline consistency.
3. Character continuity.
4. Visual identity consistency.
5. Lettering/readability.
6. Print color/finish validation.
7. AR registration testing where applicable.
8. Independent editorial pass.
9. Accessibility review.
10. Full-volume proof before print run.

## Definition of done
A volume is validated when its script, art, lettering, continuity, source lineage, print proof and optional digital companion all pass the declared production gates.

## Iteration 25 evolution
CCR TOM III and the CCR TOM IV extract extend the canon beyond the earlier trilogy framing. The project now treats CCR as a **multi-volume canon** with Oś 18 / 1679 origin, 1983/2016 dual-light motif, 2025 divergence, 2035 Shadow, 2045 memory archive, 2345 Neo-Paris, 2777 Devil Mind and infinity as explicit continuity anchors. The 33-year cycle and 16.5-year breath motif are narrative rules. Bio-photonic, consciousness-transfer and time-transmission mechanisms remain fictional world-building, not scientific claims.

The `Człowiek Roku` work is registered as a grounded narrative/biographical witness for character and 2024–2025 continuity, with its first-person claims kept distinct from independently verified evidence.
