# Project 40 — Influence Literacy & Human Agency Lab

## Purpose

Turn the supplied influence and persuasion corpus into a defensive research and product system that detects coercive or deceptive influence patterns and makes them visible to the user.

## Source-derived material

## Safe product transformation

The original material contains explicit covert-influence objectives. This project does not operationalize hidden psychological control. Instead it builds:

1. influence-pattern detection;
2. explainable UI/content annotations;
3. pressure and dependency-risk scoring;
4. transparent alternative generation;
5. media-literacy education;
6. provenance and campaign-source analysis.

## Detection pipeline

```text
Artifact / message / interface
        ↓
Feature extraction
        ↓
Pattern detectors
        ├── urgency / scarcity pressure
        ├── social proof
        ├── authority framing
        ├── commitment escalation
        ├── hidden directives
        ├── dependency loops
        └── dark-pattern signals
        ↓
Context + counter-explanations
        ↓
Risk / confidence
        ↓
Explainable report
        ↓
User-controlled mitigation
```

## Scientific discipline

Behavioral cues are not treated as deterministic proof of deception. The supplied corpus and research material support a multi-signal approach rather than a binary truth detector. The system must represent uncertainty and alternative explanations.

## Success criteria

- high recall on known manipulation patterns;
- low false-positive rate in benign persuasive content;
- explanation attached to every high-risk flag;
- user-visible provenance;
- no hidden intervention without consent.
