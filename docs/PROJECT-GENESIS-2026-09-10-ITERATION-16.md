## Safety / epistemic decisions
- Behavioral cues are not treated as proof of lying, guilt or intent.
- Reid material is retained for comparative research and false-confession analysis; automated coercion is out of scope.
- PEACE-style information gathering is the preferred interview research baseline.
- OSINT active collection and leaked-secret material remain authorization-gated defensive knowledge.
- Prompt injection is modeled as an infrastructure/authority failure, not autonomous model self-modification.
- Market forecasts and monetization claims remain hypotheses requiring current validation.
- DePIN attestation is bounded evidence, not universal truth.
- Token incentives do not establish economic sustainability without utility and anti-gaming evidence.
