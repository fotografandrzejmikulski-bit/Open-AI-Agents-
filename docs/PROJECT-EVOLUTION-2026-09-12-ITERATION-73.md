## Strategic discovery

The corpus is most valuable as a map of fragmented workflows. Repeated chains such as:

`RESEARCH → WRITING → DESIGN → VIDEO → DISTRIBUTION → SALES → ANALYTICS`

and:

`MEETING → TRANSCRIPTION → KNOWLEDGE → TASKS → SCHEDULING → FOLLOW-UP`

become candidate signals for vertical agents and workflow-native products.

## Competitive intelligence correction

Source pairings are stored as **substitution hypotheses**, not rankings. Product names, current features, pricing, availability and lifecycle require independent verification before entering a canonical provider registry.
