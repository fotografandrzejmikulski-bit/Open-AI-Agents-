## P14 / P17 / P18 — Model intelligence and routing

Add Google ecosystem findings as reinforcement for explicit separation of reasoning depth, latency, model specialization and workload class. Routing should optimize quality/cost/latency/privacy/consequence rather than select a single permanently dominant model.

## P19 / P27 / P79 / P94 — Research and discovery

Integrate the cognitive corpus's evaluation caveats: emergent-ability claims can depend on metrics; reasoning can overthink; long context can degrade retrieval; adaptation tests such as ARC-AGI stress generalization. Preserve competing hypotheses, uncertainty and replication as first-class state.

## P24 / P90 — Prompt compiler and SOP compiler

The prompt corpus strengthens the concept of prompts as structured programs: role, mission, workflow, knowledge sources, constraints, decomposition, evaluation and iterative refinement. P24 should compile these into typed, testable instruction graphs rather than treating prompts as opaque strings. P90 can compile repeatable procedures into auditable operational workflows.

OmniCore remains the convergence layer; Omnis remains the language/compiler lineage; P80 remains the self-evolving computing substrate. No split project is warranted.

## P34 / P66 / P67 — Commercialization

## P54 / P72 — Cognitive security and assurance

Use the assistant/security material to reinforce sandbox testing, guardrails, audit logs, anonymization and explicit separation between model output and authority. MCP, APIs and connectors remain capability surfaces subject to policy and authorization.

## P86 — Application/game production

Google agentic development and local/edge tooling reinforce the broader natural-language-to-build pipeline and automated verification model already established in P86.

## P91 — Synthetic persona

The assistant and cognitive corpora reinforce persona as a presentation/cognitive-style layer. Persona must not be interpreted as consciousness, identity, authority or evidence of a real person's behavior.
