## Security / ethics boundary

No new project is permitted to autonomously perform credential theft, brute force, covert intrusion, destructive cyber operations, covert psychological targeting or unauthorized influence. Source material that describes these areas is transformed into defensive requirements, threat models, controlled evaluations or explicit non-goals.
