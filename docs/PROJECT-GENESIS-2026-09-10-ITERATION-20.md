# Project Genesis — Iteration 20

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: COMPLETE

## Decision

**No new project created.**

## Reasoning

The uploaded corpus is substantial but its architectural boundaries already exist in the portfolio:

- Google AI developer ecosystem maps to model/routing/agent and sovereign-edge lineages.
- Prompt engineering and meta-prompting map to P24 and P90.
- Emergence/reasoning-limit research maps to P27/P79/P94 and assurance.
- Local-first/edge architecture maps to P61/P80/P37.
- AI assistant construction maps to P61/P90/P91.
- Sovereign digital monetization maps to P34/P66/P67.

The documents therefore increase capability density and provide additional design patterns, but do not establish a new reusable mission with a distinct architecture and verification boundary.

## Candidate explicitly rejected

**Project 95 — generic AI Prompt/Assistant Platform**

Rejected as duplicate/convergence: its proposed scope would merely combine existing P24, P61, P90 and P91 capabilities without a genuinely new boundary.

## Evidence controls

The source corpus contains strategic claims, benchmark references and case studies. These are preserved as source-derived claims or hypotheses unless independently verified. In particular, zero-cost economics, performance improvements, conversion percentages and market forecasts are not promoted to verified portfolio facts.

## Security controls

Jailbreak and guardrail material is retained for defensive assurance. Capability connectors, MCP, APIs and autonomous workflows do not acquire authorization merely by being available to a model.
