## Key synthesis

### Cognitive AI / Promptware

The two encyclopedias share a common architecture: test model limits through structured cognitive tasks, then compose new prompts from reusable dimensions. The 666 document organizes tests into six cognitive domains; the 2.0 document introduces a generator built from archetypes, contexts, constraints and output formats. The 2.0 matrix gives 20 × 20 × 10 × 6 = 24,000 theoretical combinations while labeling the curated system "2222"; the repository therefore records 2222 as a target/label, not as the mathematical output of that matrix.

The material is mapped to the existing prompt-lab and reasoning projects. It does not create a new numbered project.

### Agentic utility

The two Era Agentowej Użyteczności files are overlapping versions of the same report. The reusable contribution is an architecture centered on agentic execution, adaptive reasoning depth, inference economics, operating-system/device context, data readiness, automated oversight and monitorability. The report's claims about 2025 products, benchmarks and 2026 forecasts remain source-derived until independently verified.

### Local model engineering

The two QLoRA files describe the same end-to-end fine-tuning pattern: JSONL dataset engineering → QLoRA/LoRA adaptation → Unsloth optimization → evaluation → GGUF → Ollama. QLoRA is described using frozen 4-bit base weights, NF4 and double quantization, while LoRA learns low-rank adapters. Source-reported hardware, VRAM, speed and dataset-size figures remain benchmarks/heuristics rather than guarantees.

### Scientific frontier

The physics source is retained as a frontier-research map. Swampland, Asymptotic Safety and Dark Dimension are represented as hypotheses/frameworks linked to observations, constraints and falsification tests. Experimental anomalies and theoretical interpretations remain distinct evidence classes.

### Strategic management

Mruk's 2005 paper contributes a durable strategic-thinking layer: explicit goals, explicit rejection choices, creativity, competence fit, persistence and adaptation to environmental change. It is historical management literature and is not used as evidence for current market conditions.

### Publication engineering

The USENIX example contributes a concrete HTML/CSS/JavaScript → paged-media PDF pipeline with semantic HTML, multi-column layout, footnotes, floats, reference processing and visual publication constraints. It is treated as an engineering pattern, not as the current authoritative USENIX submission specification.

## Safety and epistemic controls

- Prompt jailbreak examples are retained as bounded evaluation stimuli, not operational bypass instructions.
- Claims of machine consciousness, genuine understanding or persistent intelligence gains remain unverified.
- Source-reported product capabilities and benchmarks remain source-derived until independently checked.
- Scientific conjectures remain hypotheses.
- Adult creative material is not transformed into an engineering knowledge artifact merely because it is present in the intake corpus.
- Duplicate documents are consolidated at the knowledge layer rather than copied into multiple project specifications.

## Project-genesis decision

**No new numbered project created.**

The corpus increases capability depth across existing lines: prompt optimization, reasoning evaluation, agentic execution, local model engineering, scientific verification, strategic/business-model engineering and professional artifact generation. Creating additional numbered projects would increase portfolio duplication without adding a distinct system boundary.
