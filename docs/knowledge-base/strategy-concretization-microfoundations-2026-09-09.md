## Durable findings
The paper distinguishes strategy from business model. The business model covers value-chain position, organizational resources, partners and relationships beyond the firm, while strategy emphasizes choices and direction of competitive development. fileciteturn211file4L153-L170

The empirical study used literature reconstruction, expert interviews, brainstorming and a self-assessment questionnaire. The research covered 77 Polish manufacturing enterprises in the agricultural-machinery sector. fileciteturn212file4L266-L318

The resulting maturity dimensions include adaptability, originality, resource configuration, opportunity creation, change management, strategic capabilities, networks, competencies and continuous deliberate implementation. The reported averages provide a useful example of turning qualitative strategic constructs into an evaluable maturity model. fileciteturn212file4L432-L525

## Engineering translation
For the repository's business-model systems, represent strategy as a versioned set of choices and business model as the operational/value architecture that realizes those choices. Track assumptions, capabilities, resources, experiments and observed outcomes separately.
