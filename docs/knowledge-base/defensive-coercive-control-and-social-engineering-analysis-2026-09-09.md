The defensive system should detect patterns without diagnosing a person from a single behavior.

## 3. Human-agency controls

For agent systems, the material translates into safeguards:

- do not exploit emotional vulnerability;
- do not manufacture dependency or exclusivity;
- do not use deception to obtain consent;
- preserve access to independent information and human support;
- make consequential decisions reversible where feasible;
- surface conflicting evidence rather than suppressing it;
- treat emotional pressure as a security signal requiring additional scrutiny.

## 4. Investment escalation and sunk-cost risk

The source describes a progression in which increasing commitments of time, money and relationships can make withdrawal psychologically costly. fileciteturn81file2L80-L89

A defensive decision system should therefore provide explicit “stop and reassess” checkpoints before irreversible commitments.
