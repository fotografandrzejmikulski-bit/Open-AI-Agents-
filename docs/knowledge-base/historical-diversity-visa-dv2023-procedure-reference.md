# Diversity Visa DV-2023 — Historical Procedure Reference

## Status
`SOURCE_DERIVED → HISTORICAL REFERENCE → NOT_CURRENT_GUIDANCE`

The supplied Polish Department of State instructions describe the DV-2023 program. They are retained as historical procedural knowledge only and must not be treated as current immigration guidance.

## 1. Program structure

The document states that DV-2023 could make up to 55,000 immigrant visas available, with allocation across six geographic regions and a country cap of seven percent. Registration itself was free. fileciteturn80file3L5-L16

## 2. Eligibility model

The historical instructions required both a qualifying country-of-chargeability pathway and either a complete secondary education equivalent or qualifying work experience. fileciteturn80file3L27-L54

The source also describes limited chargeability alternatives involving a spouse or parent, subject to specified conditions and documentation. fileciteturn82file5L227-L250

## 3. Submission integrity

For DV-2023, the source specifies an electronic submission window, one entry per participant, and disqualification for multiple entries. It also describes the DS-5501 electronic entry process. fileciteturn82file9L384-L400
