# Unsolved Problems of Science and Civilization — 2026-09-09

## Source
Nierozwiązane Problemy Świata: Kompleksowa Analiza.

## Scope
The report maps unresolved questions across physics/cosmology, chemistry, biology, medicine/neuroscience, economics, philosophy and global-risk governance. Examples include quantum gravity, dark matter/dark energy, origins and aging, room-temperature superconductivity, consciousness, free will, inequality and AI alignment. The source explicitly frames these as interacting knowledge gaps rather than an isolated list. fileciteturn211file2L74-L104

## Systems view
The most reusable idea is a **cross-domain unknowns graph**: one unresolved question can constrain another domain. The report links computational complexity, biological structure, cosmology, consciousness and governance into a common research-planning problem.

## AI/research implication
An advanced research agent should represent:
- problem statement;
- known evidence;
- competing hypotheses;
- unresolved variables;
- dependencies on other open problems;
- candidate experiments/simulations;
- falsification criteria;
- confidence and provenance.

The report also identifies AI governance/alignment as an unresolved systems problem, alongside geopolitical fragmentation and collective-action barriers. fileciteturn211file7L265-L282

## Epistemic boundary
The document is a survey/report, not evidence that any listed problem has been solved. Hypotheses and speculative mechanisms remain hypotheses.
