# Sovereign Digital Economy, Revenue Architecture & Agentic Finance — Source-Derived Module

## Status
`SOURCE_DERIVED → SPECULATIVE/EMERGING → NOT_VERIFIED`

Two supplied files are materially overlapping versions of the same report, **Monetyzacja Ekstremalna: Architektura Suwerennej Ekonomii Cyfrowej**. They are therefore represented once rather than duplicated.

## 1. Core thesis of the source

The source describes a shift from token-emission-driven incentives toward revenue-driven digital economic systems. It identifies four converging pillars: protocol-owned liquidity / proof of liquidity, MEV/OEV value capture, AI-agent/DeFAI economics and real-world-asset tokenization. fileciteturn82file4L189-L210

## 2. Revenue layers

The source groups revenue mechanisms into:

1. **Protocol Fees** — fees for use of protocol functionality.
2. **Internalized Volatility** — capture associated with liquidation/arbitrage and MEV.
3. **Asset Yield** — yield generated by treasury assets, including RWA and staking. fileciteturn82file8L343-L365

These should be modeled separately because they have different risk, liquidity, regulatory and operational properties.

## 3. Agent-native economy

The source proposes an eventual shift in which AI agents become important users of digital services and protocols, creating demand for APIs, data access, execution rights and machine-oriented products. This is a forward-looking hypothesis, not an established prediction. fileciteturn81file1L43-L50

## 4. RWA as an external-value bridge

The report treats tokenized real-world assets as a mechanism for connecting on-chain treasury systems to off-chain economic yield. It describes SPV-based representations and examples involving treasuries and private credit. These examples are source-derived and require independent current verification before investment or implementation decisions. fileciteturn81file1L51-L69

## 5. Legal and governance boundary

The source itself recognizes that a purportedly sovereign digital economic system still interacts with banking, employment, intellectual property and other off-chain institutions. Therefore “digital sovereignty” must not be modeled as exemption from law or regulation. fileciteturn82file8L363-L368

## 6. Engineering model

A reusable treasury architecture can be represented as:

```text
USER / AGENT ACTIVITY
       ↓
PROTOCOL SERVICE
       ↓
FEES / VALUE CAPTURE
       ↓
TREASURY
   ┌───┼────┐
   ↓   ↓    ↓
LIQUIDITY  RWA  RESERVES
   ↓   ↓    ↓
RISK / POLICY / ACCOUNTING
       ↓
AUDITED ALLOCATION
       ↓
MEASURED OUTCOME
```

Every autonomous financial action should remain subject to authorization, risk limits, accounting, postcondition verification and regulatory review.

## 7. Epistemic controls

Claims about “infinite” self-optimization, superior autonomous capital management, projected market sizes, yields or dominance are hypotheses/source claims unless independently evidenced. The repository does not promote the report's rhetoric into verified financial fact.
