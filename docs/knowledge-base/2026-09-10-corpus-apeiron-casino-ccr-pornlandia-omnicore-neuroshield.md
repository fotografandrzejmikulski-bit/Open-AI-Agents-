## 1. Apeiron artifact lineage
The Apeiron 2.0 report describes a 78-card Analog AR artifact whose semantics emerge from transparent layered cards. It specifies optical-grade transparent polymer, approximately 0.30 mm thickness, controlled surface friction, hybrid opaque/translucent/transparent printing, white-underprint levels, digital embellishment, a Light Matrix Board and a System Kernel Manual.

The Cyberpunk Tarot document describes a distinct 60-card visual system: 22-card System Core plus 38 Subroutines, with Neon / Chrome / Data / Wires as the four thematic suits and glitch/datamoshing/pixel-sorting as the visual language.

Black System Apeiron 2.2 extends the Apeiron lineage with black/glitch presentation, thermochromic and UV reveal layers and a Codex-style manual. The supplied Aperion-vs-Black-System document additionally proposes QR-fragment collection and explicitly manipulative visual mechanisms.

### Portfolio impact
- **Project 71** is the canonical physical-artifact owner. It was evolved with optical-layer, reveal-state and manufacturing-QC requirements.
- Projects 01/05/50/53/57/71 remain related lineages.
- No new Apeiron project is created because the supplied material is strongly covered by the existing physical-artifact lineage.

### Epistemic boundary
Material specifications such as light transmission, friction, durability and production tolerances remain targets until supplier-batch tests reproduce them. A design document is not physical validation.

## 2. Casino architecture and cognitive manipulation corpus
The casino report covers microservice architecture, relational/NoSQL/in-memory storage, RNG, provably-fair schemes, house edge, Martingale, Kelly criterion, advantage play and a proposed Neuro-Casino using behavioral manipulation, NLP, biometric feedback and sensory effects.

### Portfolio impact
- **Project 78** gains a broader statistical-audit context: RNG, provably-fair verification, house edge and simulation.
- **Projects 51/54/72/79** gain adversarial and cognitive-security relevance.
- No project is created to operationalize manipulation of gamblers.

### Safety boundary
The repository may study casino systems defensively, audit randomness and model risk, but it does not convert the supplied coercive/manipulative Neuro-Casino concepts into deployment instructions for exploiting users.

## 3. CCR graphic-novel adaptation
The CCR report defines a multi-volume graphic-novel adaptation of the trilogy, with light as a semantic/narrative protagonist. It defines timelines including 1679, 1983, 2025, 2135, 2345, 2777 and infinity, each with distinct visual identities. It also specifies characters, CCR technology, equations as visual elements, fluid paneling, synesthetic presentation and a pilot chapter.

The supplied pilot begins in Cieszyn in 2025, transitions through a mine-memory sequence and then to a 2135 CCR laboratory, ending with the appearance of the Shadow. The document proposes approximately 12 volumes of 120–160 pages and a transmedia/AR layer.

### Portfolio impact
- **Project 73** remains the game-specific Człowiek Roku lineage and should absorb relevant evidence/provenance constraints where the same story material is used.
- A separate **Project 82** is justified for the complete CCR graphic-novel/transmedia production product because the medium, deliverable and production pipeline are distinct from the game runtime.

## 4. Baśń o Pornlandii graphic-novel adaptation
The supplied report specifies a graphic-novel adaptation centered on a dystopian world in which the environment itself functions as an antagonist. It emphasizes biomorphic sterile architecture, mercury imagery, cold neon versus historical warmth, Rocco, Mira and Andrzej, faceless enforcers, gutter semiotics, silence/missing senses and multi-volume visual storytelling.

The report also contains OSINT-derived statements about the author. Such statements are treated as source claims and provenance-tagged, not as established facts.

### Portfolio impact
A separate **Project 83** is justified because this is a distinct graphic-novel product and world-building pipeline, not merely a component of CCR or Project 73.

## 5. Aksamitna Sieć / Velvet Lattice
The board-game document proposes a 3–6 player, 60–90 minute noir strategy game centered on a physical Whispering Tower, influence tokens, asymmetric social roles and language-mediated interaction. The document explicitly frames variable-ratio rewards, compulsion loops, hidden persuasion and manipulative marketing as design goals.

### Portfolio impact
No existing repository artifact owns the complete product concept. A new **Project 84** is justified, but only as a transparent social-strategy game architecture. The physical tower, asymmetric roles, social interaction and dramatic uncertainty can be retained; covert psychological manipulation, subliminal commands, addictive optimization and coercive marketing are excluded from production requirements.

## 7. NeuroShield-GeneBlock / epilepsy research
The NeuroShield report proposes a theoretical disease-modifying strategy aimed at early epileptogenesis, combining multiple mechanistic targets, gene-delivery concepts, blood-brain-barrier delivery, biomarker discovery and translational risk analysis.

The document discusses neuroinflammation, synaptic remodeling, inhibitory signaling, NPY pathways, biomarkers such as HFOs, imaging and molecular signals, vector immunogenicity and long-term safety.

### Portfolio impact
A separate **Project 85** is justified as a research-and-verification program because the corpus defines a specific therapeutic hypothesis, diagnostic strategy and translational roadmap not owned by the generic scientific-discovery project.

Project 85 must remain a research architecture, not a clinical protocol. Any therapeutic candidate requires staged target validation, preclinical evidence, safety review, regulatory oversight and clinical investigation. The repository does not treat the proposed construct as an established treatment.

## 8. Cross-corpus synthesis
The most important convergence is not another isolated artifact. It is a reusable pipeline:

```text
SOURCE CORPUS
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
PROJECT IMPACT
    ↓
NEW PRODUCT / RESEARCH GENESIS
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

```

Three cross-domain patterns are especially strong:

### A. Physical artifacts as information systems
Apeiron's layered cards, Black System reveal states, QR/meta-game concepts and physical board mechanics extend Project 71 from manufacturing into physical information-state engineering.

### B. Probabilistic systems require independent authority
Casino RNG, game randomness, AI narrative proposals and learned OmniCore components all reinforce the same invariant: a probabilistic generator may propose or produce an outcome, but authoritative state and consequential actions must be governed by independent rules and verification.

### C. Creative worlds need production-grade provenance
CCR and Pornlandia require a structured separation between source material, adaptation decisions, visual invention, OSINT claims and established facts. This aligns with Projects 19, 32, 54, 72 and 73.

## 9. Global safety/epistemic boundaries
- Manipulation mechanisms in the source corpus are retained as analytical objects, not production objectives.
- Gambling analysis is defensive/statistical, not a promise of guaranteed winnings.
- Medical material is a hypothesis/research architecture, not a treatment recommendation or clinical protocol.
- OSINT claims about real people remain provenance-tagged.
- Speculative physics/mathematics remain speculative until independently established.
- Physical manufacturing parameters require batch testing.
- Generated AI code remains untrusted until compilation, testing and assurance.
