# Physical Symbolic Artifact Engineering — Apeiron / Black System / Game Production

## Source corpus
Consolidates the supplied Apeiron 2.0, Black Apeiron 2.2 and game-production documents. It extends the existing physical-game and symbolic-interface knowledge rather than replacing it.

## Physical interface thesis
Apeiron 2.0 deliberately removes electronics and treats polymer, ink, optical layering, perception and mechanical handling as the interface substrate. The supplied RC document specifies a 78-card transparent stack, approximately 0.30 mm card thickness and a high-transmission optical substrate as design requirements. These values remain supplier/material specifications until laboratory validation.

## Layered rendering
The physical artifact can encode state through:
- transparency/opacity;
- white-underprint topology;
- thermochromic reveal;
- UV/fluorescent layers;
- tactile finishes;
- registration and overlay geometry;
- controlled visual distortion.

The key abstraction is a declarative physical-renderer profile rather than manually authored one-off production instructions.

## Black Apeiron 2.2
The Black Apeiron corpus reframes the transparent interface into black-box/void aesthetics and a defensive metaphor of hiddenness. The useful engineering extraction is not literal "spiritual cryptography" but the concept of **privacy-by-design physical state**: valuable or sensitive information may be deliberately unavailable to casual observation.

## Symbolic boundary
Tarot/divination symbolism is a reflective and interpretive layer. The artifact must never claim empirical diagnosis, guaranteed prediction or factual authority solely from symbolic combinations.

## Quality model
Record at minimum:
`dimensions, thickness, transmission, haze, registration, surface friction, durability, reveal reliability, accessibility, batch ID, supplier/material lot, design hash`.

## Accessibility
Essential state must have non-color and non-visual alternatives. Physical effects are allowed to enrich interaction, but cannot become hidden behavioral controls.

## Defensive influence boundary
The Black Apeiron and casino/psychology sources contain material about covert commands, manipulation and attention capture. Those mechanisms become threat-model metadata and evaluation cases only. Product behavior must remain explicit, disclosed, reversible and user-controlled.
