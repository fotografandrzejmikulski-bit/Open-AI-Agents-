## Durable knowledge
The corpus surveys open mathematical problems including the Clay Millennium problems, Riemann Hypothesis, P vs NP, Collatz, Navier–Stokes, Yang–Mills and related frontiers. It repeatedly emphasizes that numerical experiments and apparent empirical success do not constitute a proof. One supplied report explicitly states that, as of its December 2025 framing, P vs NP remained open and that superficial AI-generated proofs require stronger verification. Source: Nierozwiązane Problemy Matematyczne — pp. 1–2; Nowa Matematyka: Hipoteza Riemanna i P vs NP — end of report.

## Verification boundary
The Principia/TRS documents must be stored as **source-derived speculative theory**, not as established mathematics. They assert that mathematical objects can be modeled as resonators in a spectral space Ω and propose an Energy–Complexity principle and phase-based interpretations of prime structure. fileciteturn210file0L17-L22

Some source passages go further and label P ≠ NP, RH, Navier–Stokes and other problems as solved or reformulated within TRS. Those statements are claims made by the supplied documents, not repository-verified mathematical results. The repository must preserve this distinction.
