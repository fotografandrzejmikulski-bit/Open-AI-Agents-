The two `Kod Systemu Wieloagentowego (MAS) dla Androida` files are not byte-identical; the second is an expanded/corroborating implementation witness. Together with the general Android forensic report it justifies P106.

## Epistemic and safety boundaries
- MAS action capability requires explicit authorization and postcondition verification.
- Accessibility/ADB control is not equivalent to unrestricted device authority.
- Forensic exploit/bypass references remain defensive research context.
- Legal/credential-extraction implications are not operationalized.
- Generated documentary imagery must not be represented as authentic evidence of events that did not occur.
- Historical photographic references are descriptive visual-language inputs, not permission to falsely attribute generated work.
- META AGENT is a curriculum/catalogue, not proof of capability.
- Microgrant funding terms are source-specific and may change; no external endorsement is inferred.
