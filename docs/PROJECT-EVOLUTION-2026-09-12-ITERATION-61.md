## Highest-value discoveries

1. SEO is packaged as a closed-loop autonomous growth system rather than isolated keyword/content tools.
2. AI-answer visibility is treated as a separate optimization surface from classic SERP ranking.
3. Competitive research can feed directly into an executable content roadmap.
4. Publishing, backlink acquisition and measurement form a recurring agentic workflow.
5. Outcome-oriented metrics are more valuable than raw content volume.
6. Autonomous external actions require explicit authorization and readback controls.

## New invariants

- `AI SEARCH VISIBILITY ≠ CLASSIC SERP RANKING`
- `MODEL CITATION ≠ AUTHORITY PROOF`
- `SHARE OF MODEL ≠ UNIVERSAL STANDARD`
- `BACKLINK AUTOMATION ≠ SAFE BY DEFAULT`
- `CONTENT VOLUME ≠ TOPICAL AUTHORITY`
- `TRAFFIC ≠ REVENUE`
- `VENDOR CASE STUDY ≠ INDEPENDENT BENCHMARK`
- `AUTONOMOUS PUBLISHING ≠ AUTHORIZATION`
- `AUTOPILOT ≠ UNSUPERVISED AUTHORITY`

## Research doctrine preserved

Vendor claims are stored as claims, not silently promoted to verified facts. Metrics remain distinct from causality and business outcomes. External actions remain governed by:

`OBSERVE → PLAN → AUTHORIZE → ACT → READBACK → VERIFY`
