## New strategic invariant
`product hypothesis → business model → strategy → experiment → evidence → controlled decision → implementation → measurement → adaptation`

- ambitious product goals must coexist with cost control;
- important customer segments must be explicit;
- failed experiments become durable learning;
- strategy must remain flexible and communicable;
- business-model architecture and competitive strategy must remain distinguishable;
- metrics are evidence of observed outcomes, not automatic causal explanations.
