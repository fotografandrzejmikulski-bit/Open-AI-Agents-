## Cross-project architectural consequences

1. Model capability remains distinct from authorization.
2. Memory and semantic retrieval remain distinct from authority.
3. Generated code remains untrusted until tested.
4. AI-generated patches require authoritative postcondition checks.
5. Simulation/digital twins remain models, not real-world evidence.
6. Self-healing is constrained candidate generation + validation, not unrestricted kernel mutation.
7. Research agents may recommend actions but do not become authorization authorities.
8. Security research is used to strengthen the control plane rather than to create bypass tooling.

## Decision

`NO NEW PROJECT`

The batch materially strengthens Projects 61, 80, 87, 19, 27, 79, 94, 54, 72, 77, 34, 66 and 67, but does not establish a distinct mission/architecture/verification boundary beyond existing lineages.
