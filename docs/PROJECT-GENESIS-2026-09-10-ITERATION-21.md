## Genesis decision

### Project 95
**Omni-Entity Sovereign Network & Cyber-Resilience Fabric MAX**

### Why the boundary is independent

The source corpus combines infrastructure sovereignty, distributed mesh networking, cryptographic node/service identity, anti-Sybil admission, defensive monitoring and continuity/recovery at an entity/network scale. This is not merely another edge-compute runtime and is not reducible to node hardware attestation.

- P37 = sovereign edge runtime and multi-model delivery.
- P93 = hardware-rooted identity, attestation and physical/computational work evidence.
- P95 = distributed entity/network resilience and service continuity across failure domains.

### Architecture boundary

P95 owns:

- network topology and failure-domain modeling;
- resilient overlay/mesh connectivity;
- cryptographic identity and trust-state lifecycle;
- admission/revocation/anti-Sybil controls;
- defensive telemetry and anomaly correlation;
- incident isolation and recovery;
- evidence continuity across distributed infrastructure.

P95 does not own:

- offensive intrusion;
- destructive operations;
- covert persistence/evasion;
- credential theft;
- unauthorized surveillance;
- autonomous geopolitical/legal action.

## Source evidence

The Omni-Entity report explicitly proposes mesh networks, cryptographic identity, post-quantum cryptography/QKD, honeypots and anti-Sybil mechanisms as security pillars, and compares Yggdrasil, B.A.T.M.A.N. and CJDNS as candidate network technologies.

These recommendations are retained as source-derived architecture candidates and must be benchmarked before adoption.

## Verification requirements

Project 95 cannot be promoted beyond architecture baseline until a controlled testbed demonstrates:

1. tolerance to node/link failure;
2. measurable behavior under network partition;
3. cryptographic identity lifecycle and revocation;
4. bounded blast radius after simulated compromise;
5. anti-Sybil admission effectiveness under synthetic adversarial load;
6. evidence and telemetry integrity;
7. reproducible service recovery.

## Safety decision

All offensive cybersecurity material is transformed into defensive threat-model requirements. No operational attack chain is promoted to a capability of the project.
